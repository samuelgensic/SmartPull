Smart·Pull Music Studio

Each track's current version is next to the new one, at matched loudness. The new tracks keep changing: their melodies grow from remembered motifs, and their chords, textures and keys shift every minute or so. Give each one two or three minutes, or turn on Hear changes sooner.

Headphones or a good speaker help. For AirPlay or a HomePod, connect it first, then open or reload this page, then press play. On iPhone, the ring/silent switch doesn't mute this page. Piano: Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm (CC BY 3.0).
